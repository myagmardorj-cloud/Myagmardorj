Prime-Correlated Structure
in High Riemann Zero Blocks

Myagmardorj Namnansuren · Nexcore LTD · Ulaanbaatar
Numerical evidence for the Bogomolny–Keating amplitude law A(p) ∼ C(log p)²/p in high Riemann zero blocks. Tested across three independent Odlyzko datasets at heights T ~ 10¹², 10¹³. Correlation r ≥ 0.94 in all cases. This is not a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis.

✓
Prime-location excess confirmed: 12/12 prime positions (p = 2, 3, 5, …, 37) show positive excess A(p) > 0 across all three datasets.
✓
BK amplitude law confirmed: Pearson r ≥ 0.94 on all datasets. Ratio R(p) = A(p)/B(p) ≈ 16.5 is approximately constant across primes.
✗
Not a proof of RH: This is a numerical observation only. Theoretical derivation of the constant C ≈ 16.5 is needed.
