Numerical Observations on Prime-Correlated Structure in High Riemann Zero Blocks
Myagmardorj Namnansuren · Nexcore LTD · Ulaanbaatar
Computational observations consistent with the Bogomolny–Keating amplitude law A(p) ∼ C·(log p)²/p in high Riemann zero spacing covariance. Tested across three independent Odlyzko datasets. Not a confirmed theorem, not a proof of RH — an exploratory computational study.
