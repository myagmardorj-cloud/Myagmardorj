# Mellin Operator Framework – Товч тайлбар

## Hilbert space
H = L²((0,∞), dx/x)

## Mellin transform
(Mf)(τ) = (1/√(2π)) ∫₀^∞ f(x) x^{-iτ} dx/x

## Prime operator
(P_σ f)(x) = Σ_{n≥1} Λ(n)/n^σ · f(x/n)

## Multiplier theorem
M[P_σ f](τ) = -ζ'(σ+iτ)/ζ(σ+iτ) · Mf(τ)

## Energy identity
E(σ,ε,X) = ∫_R |ζ'/ζ(σ+iτ)|² |φ̂(τ)|² dτ
