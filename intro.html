The Riemann Hypothesis is one of 7 unsolved Millennium Prize Problems. Only 1 has been solved (Poincaré, 2003). Our work reports a numerical pattern consistent with BK-type predictions — not a confirmed theorem, not a proof of RH, and not yet a recognized research contribution.
